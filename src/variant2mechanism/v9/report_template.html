04 / ARCHIVE

Rejected, deferred and superseded

Rejection applies to one hypothesis version, after a required claim was contradicted in its scope. Deferral is a budget or evidence decision, not a refutation. Claims a rejected hypothesis shared stay available. When the contradiction behind a rejection is later withdrawn, code reinstates the hypothesis as deferred, once; a second rejection is final, and is then shown as a rejection that no longer stands.
