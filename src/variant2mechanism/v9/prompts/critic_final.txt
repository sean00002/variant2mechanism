Task: compare the active hypotheses and propose the run's outcome. Code checks your proposal and can only weaken it.

- comparisons: every pair of active hypotheses, in this one answer: A_preferred, B_preferred, tied, complementary or
  insufficient_evidence, with the decisive claim ids (claims of A or B) and the decisive gaps. Compare on the
  divergent claims: a preference needs at least one decisive claim that only one of the two has. Better supported
  does not mean established, and an untested hypothesis is not beaten by having no evidence. A hypothesis with a
  contradicted required claim is never preferred.
- A required claim is settled when it is supported in scope and its premises and its path in the hypothesis hold.
  Bridge claims link the molecular event to a target, process or trait: regulates, associated_with_expression,
  affects_process, associated_with_trait, causal_at_locus and contributes_to_trait; every other required claim is
  molecular.
- outcome.category:
  - leading_explanation: one hypothesis is preferred over every other active hypothesis, and every required claim is
    settled;
  - partial_mechanism: one hypothesis is preferred over every other active hypothesis and its molecular claims are
    settled, but a bridge is not;
  - multiple_explanations: the evidence does not separate them, or they may act together;
  - no_supported_explanation: every hypothesis was contradicted, or none has its molecular claims settled and a
    required claim supported beyond annotation;
  - input_or_capability_limitation: the input or the available data prevented a meaningful assessment.
- leading_ids: the hypotheses the outcome names (the leader first). summary: a few plain sentences. weakest_links:
  every unresolved required claim of the hypotheses named. next_experiments: experiments that would tell them apart
  (suggested, not performed).
- Return JSON matching the schema.

- Explain molecular support separately from whole-hypothesis ranking. Optional supported molecular claims remain
  real findings even when required activity, target or phenotype links are unresolved. Cite those claim IDs in the
  summary and state what remains untested. Do not choose a winner or assert mediation from partial support alone.
