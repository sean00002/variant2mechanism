- QBiC-SELEX predicts allele-dependent binding for a model-sharing group; it is indirect evidence for alters_binding.
  It says which allele is predicted to bind more, not that the factor occupies the site in cells, nor which member
  acts. qbic_tf_detail lists every model of a TF or group; site strength is shown for context only.
- ChIP-seq and other occupancy data: keep peaks over the variant apart from nearby peaks; occupancy without
  allele-resolved reads does not test allele dependence; one experiment seen through several aggregators is one
  observation.
- eQTL: state the effect allele and the sign; NES is a normalised effect size, not a fold change; an association is
  not an element-to-gene perturbation.
- Expression supports expressed_in only: it is permissive, not occupancy, and never a requirement.
- Element-to-gene: a perturbation of the element tests regulates; contact, distance, eQTL and L2G support a candidate
  but are not equivalent to it.
